## 💡 Executive Summary & Key Insights

* **Dominant Sales Category:** Clothing leads overall demand, capturing **63%** of all units sold, followed by Furniture (**18%**) and Electronics (**18%**).
* **Payment Preference:** Cash on Delivery (COD) remains the primary checkout mode at **55%**, with digital and card payments (Debit Card at 18%, Credit Card at 13%, EMI at 11%) making up the remainder.
* **Profit Driver by Sub-Category:** High-margin office tech (Printers) generates the bulk of net profit, offsetting thinner margins on lower-cost apparel items.
* **Monthly Volatility:** High positive margins recorded in January, followed by moderated profit in February, and an operational margin squeeze in March.

## ⚙️ Interactive Functionality

1. **Quarterly Performance Slicers (Qtr 1 – Qtr 4):**
   * Top-level button matrix allowing single-click filtering across entire metrics and visuals for each individual quarter.
2. **Geographical Drilldown:**
   * Dynamic **State dropdown** to isolate regional performance (e.g., tracking specific sales volume, top customers, and margin within Uttar Pradesh).
3. **Core KPI Cards:**
   * **Total Sales Amount:** Immediate revenue visibility (e.g., ₹17K in selected view).
   * **Net Profit:** Profitability tracking (e.g., ₹2K in selected view).
   * **Quantity Sold:** Order volume monitoring (e.g., 141 units).
   * **Average Order Value (AOV):** Spend value per order (e.g., ₹3K).

## 📊 Visualizations Included

| Visual | Type | Business Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sum of Amount by State** | Horizontal Bar Chart | Identifies highest grossing geographical regions. |
| **Profit by Month** | Highlight Column Chart | Tracks seasonal trends and detects loss-making periods. |
| **Quantity by Category** | Donut Chart | Monitors inventory distribution and consumer category demand. |
| **Quantity by Payment Mode** | Donut Chart | Measures customer checkout preferences and COD dependence. |
| **Amount by Customer Name** | Bar Chart | Highlights high-value accounts (e.g., Harivansh, Madan Mohan). |
| **Profit by Sub-Category** | Ranked Horizontal Bar | Surfaces high-margin vs low-margin merchandise lines. |
